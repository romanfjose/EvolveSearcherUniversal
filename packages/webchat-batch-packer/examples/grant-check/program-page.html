Open Science Fund

The Open Science Fund supports independent research teams publishing open data and open-source tools.

Awards range from $25,000 to $150,000 for projects lasting up to 18 months.

Applicants must be registered nonprofits, universities, or fiscally sponsored teams in any country.

Applications are submitted through the online portal; the next deadline is 2027-02-15 at 17:00 UTC.

Funded projects must release outputs under an OSI-approved or CC BY 4.0 license.
